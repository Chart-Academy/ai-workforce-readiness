Agent Lab WO-06 · Everyone

Calendar Manager

AI for Workforce Readiness · Chart Academy

The job

What this agent does

  • Collect plans from texts, emails, and notes
  • Find conflicts before they happen
  • Build a realistic week

At work

Who uses this

  • Anyone juggling shifts and family
  • Job seekers with several interviews
  • Office staff who schedule for others

How agents work

Four parts of every agent

  1. InstructionsIts job description: goal, rules, format
  2. InputsThe files and facts you give it
  3. OutputThe table, list, or draft it gives back
  4. You checkYou are responsible for the final result

Build it

Set up your agent

  1. Open your AI assistant and your calendar app
  2. Paste the starter instructions from the Agent Labs page
  3. Fill in your fixed commitments
  4. Explain in your own words what a good week looks like for you

Practice data · on the Agent Labs page

Your practice files

  • wo06-schedule-notes-messy.txt

These files have mistakes in them on purpose. Look for:

  • Two things at the same time
  • Missing AM/PM or unclear dates
  • Different time zones
  • The same appointment listed twice

Check it

Before you trust the output

  • Every conflict is listed before the plan
  • Unclear items become questions, not guesses
  • Times are converted to your time zone
  • Deadlines get a reminder the day before

Ethics & accountability

Use it responsibly

  • Ask before sharing other people's schedules
  • Confirm unclear times with the person who sent them
  • Tell your manager early about conflicts
