Warehouse Associate — Inventory & Shipping
Riverbend Distribution Co. · Springfield · Full time · $18.50–$20.00/hour

About the role
Riverbend Distribution supplies grocery stores and restaurants across the region. Warehouse Associates receive deliveries, keep inventory accurate, and pick and pack customer orders on time.

What you'll do
- Receive, count, and check incoming shipments against purchase orders
- Use a handheld scanner to update inventory in our warehouse system
- Pick and pack orders accurately (99% accuracy goal)
- Operate pallet jacks and, after training, forklifts
- Keep work areas clean and follow safety procedures
- Communicate with drivers and team leads about delays or damaged goods

What we're looking for
- High school diploma or GED
- 1+ year in retail, warehouse, auto service, moving, or another hands-on job
- Comfortable lifting up to 50 lbs
- Basic computer skills; experience with scanners or point-of-sale systems a plus
- Forklift certification preferred (we will recertify)
- Bilingual English/Spanish a plus
- Reliable attendance and attention to detail

Schedule: Monday–Friday, 6:00 AM – 2:30 PM. Some Saturdays during the holidays.
Benefits: Health insurance after 60 days, paid time off, tuition help.
Open interviews every Tuesday, 9 AM – 12 PM, at 4100 Industrial Pkwy. Bring a résumé.
