Final Project: Your Artificial Intelligence (AI) Workforce

Put together a small team of agents that supports your own job goal, and show it working. You build it during the last class sessions, not at home.

What to build

  1. Pick a goal. Land a first job, return to work, or get better at the job you have.
  2. Choose three agents from the Agent Labs (or design your own) that help with that goal.
  3. Improve each agent. Test it on real tasks, note what went wrong, and update its instructions.
  4. Present a 5-minute demo showing the agents working together on one real task, from start to finish.

What to upload to Canvas

  • The final instructions for each of your three agents
  • Before-and-after examples showing how each agent improved
  • A one-page reflection in your own words. Use these starters: “The goal my agents support is …”, “The ethical choice I made was …”, “The biggest mistake an agent made was … and I caught it by …”
  • A source list for any facts your agents gave you
