How work is scored

Grading

There is no homework. You're graded on in-class work submitted in Canvas, and on whether you can explain it in your own words.

How each assignment is scored

Every assignment is worth 10 points and has its own rubric, built from its steps. Each rubric row is scored Full, Partial, or Not yet, and the last row is always your In your own words answers. Open an assignment to see its rubric. The same rubric appears in Canvas.

How your work is reviewed

An Artificial Intelligence (AI) tool gives each submission a first score using that assignment's rubric and leaves a short comment on each rubric row. A teacher reviews every grade before you see it. Teachers change scores when needed, and anything the AI is unsure about goes straight to a teacher.

  • Submit your work in Canvas using the Submit in Canvas button in each lesson or lab.
  • If your Google link isn't shared as Anyone with the link → Viewer, your work can't be read and isn't graded until you share it.
  • Remove your address, phone number, and email from a résumé before you submit it.

Readiness Check and growth

You take the Readiness Check twice: on day one and as Part A of the Final Assessment.

  • Day one isn't graded. It only shows where you're starting.
  • Part A is graded on your score. Your growth from day one is reported to you and the program.
  • Part B, the Job Placement Capstone, has its own 10-point rubric with a row for each of its five parts. See it under How each assignment is scored.

Finish strong

Certificate and LinkedIn badge

Finish the course with 80% proficiency or higher to earn a Satisfactory Completion Certificate and a digital badge for your LinkedIn profile.

  • Submit every assignment in Canvas, including the Agent Labs and the Final Assessment.
  • Reach 80% or higher for the whole course, using the weights above.
  • Show it to employers. List the certificate on your résumé, and add the badge to your LinkedIn profile under Licenses & certifications.
