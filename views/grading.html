How work is scored

Grading

There is no homework. You're graded on in-class work uploaded to Canvas, and on whether you can explain it in your own words.

ComponentWhat countsWeight
ParticipationAttendance and working with classmates15%
Lesson assessmentsIn-class assessments for Lessons 1–10, uploaded to Canvas20%
Agent Lab assessmentsIn-class assessments for Work Orders 01 through 06 (WO-01 through WO-06), uploaded to Canvas40%
Final AssessmentPart A: Readiness Check (10%). Part B: Applied Tasks (15%).25%
Total100%

How your work is reviewed

An Artificial Intelligence (AI) tool gives each submission a first score using the rubric above and leaves a short comment for each part. A teacher reviews every grade before you see it. Teachers change scores when needed, and anything the AI is unsure about goes straight to a teacher.

  • Submit your work in Canvas using the Submit in Canvas button in each lesson or lab.
  • If your Google link isn't shared as Anyone with the link → Viewer, your work can't be read and isn't graded until you share it.
  • Remove your address, phone number, and email from a résumé before you submit it.

Readiness Check and growth

You take the Readiness Check twice: on day one and as Part A of the Final Assessment.

  • Day one isn't graded. It only shows where you're starting.
  • Part A is graded on your score. Your growth from day one is reported to you and the program.
  • Part B uses the same scoring as every in-class assessment: did it, checked it, own words.

Completion certificate

Upload every assessment to Canvas and finish the course with 70% or higher to earn a certificate of completion you can list on your résumé.
