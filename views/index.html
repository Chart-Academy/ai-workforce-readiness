Artificial Intelligence for Workforce Readiness

Built for people looking for their first job, returning to work, or growing in their current career. Learn to use Artificial Intelligence (AI) responsibly, then put it to work on your job search: sharpen your résumé, match your skills to real openings, prepare for interviews, and stay organized, so you move faster into a good, stable job. No coding experience needed.

Start here See the agents you'll build

Who this course is for

Wherever you're starting from, the goal is the same: a job that pays well, fits your life, and has room to grow.

Path A

Looking for your first job

Turn your school, volunteer, and life experience into a strong résumé, find openings that fit your schedule and hire quickly, and practice until you're ready for your first interview.

Path B

Returning to work

Explain a gap in your work history with confidence, catch up on the tools employers expect, and match the skills you already have, including caregiving and volunteering, to real openings.

Path C

Growing in your current career

Save hours each week on email, scheduling, and record-keeping, show your employer you can work well with AI, and prepare for a promotion or a better-paying role.

What you'll leave with

  • A résumé tailored to real job postings, built from experience you actually have.
  • A list of real openings matched to you: your pay, schedule, and commute, including employers that hire quickly.
  • An application tracker and follow-up plan, so no opportunity slips through the cracks.
  • Interview practice with feedback, and honest, confident answers about gaps or career changes.
  • Six AI agents you built yourself to keep your job search moving after the course ends.
  • Skills employers are hiring for: using AI safely and honestly, checking facts, protecting private information, and spotting job scams.
