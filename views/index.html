Who this course is for

Path A

Starting your first job

Build a résumé from your school, volunteer, and life experience, find openings that hire quickly, and walk into your first interview prepared.

Path B

Returning to work

Catch up on the tools employers now expect, explain a gap in your work history with confidence, and turn the skills you already have into job matches.

Path C

Growing in your current job

Save hours each week on email, scheduling, research, and record-keeping, and show your employer you can work well with AI.

What you'll leave with

  • A clear understanding of how AI tools work, where they make mistakes, and how to check their answers.
  • Personal rules for using AI ethically and citing where information came from.
  • Six working AI agents you built yourself, from a research assistant to an interview coach.
  • An updated résumé, a skills profile, and a list of openings matched to you.
