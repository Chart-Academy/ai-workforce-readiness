Before the first class

Getting Started

You need a computer or tablet, an internet connection, and a free Artificial Intelligence (AI) account. Everything else we set up together.

Set up your accounts

  1. An email address you are comfortable giving to employers. If yours is a nickname, make a new one with your first and last name.
  2. A free AI assistant account. Claude, ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, or Google Gemini all work for this course.
  3. A cloud drive (Google Drive or OneDrive) to keep your résumé, notes, and agent instructions in one place.
  4. A calendar (Google Calendar or Outlook) for the calendar agent lab.

How each class works

There is no homework. All work happens in class and is turned in before you leave.

  • Learn. A short lesson on the topic, with slides on the Lessons page.
  • Build. Hands-on time with practice files that have mistakes in them on purpose. Finding the mistakes is part of the job.
  • Assess. Finish the in-class assessment and upload it to Canvas so your work is saved in one place.

Your words, your work

Every assessment ends with an In your own words section. AI can help you build and check things, but this part you write yourself. Employers want to know what you understand, and so do we.

Each one gives you sentence starters to get going, like:

  • “The biggest mistake the AI made was … I caught it by …”
  • “If I used this at work, I would still need to check …”

Short and honest beats long and polished. A few clear sentences is enough.

Ground rules for using AI in this class

  • Never paste your Social Security number, bank details, passwords, or medical records into an AI tool.
  • Check any fact that matters before you use it. AI can sound confident and still be wrong.
  • Say when you used AI, and cite the sources it relied on.
  • Write the “In your own words” part of every assessment yourself.
  • Your résumé and interview answers must describe things you actually did.

New to computers or out of the workforce for a while? That's who this course was built for. Ask questions early and often.
