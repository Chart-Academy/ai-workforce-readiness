Skills-to-Openings Agent

AI for Workforce Readiness · Chart Academy

The job

What this agent does

  • Turn your experience into a clear skills profile
  • Match you to real openings
  • Spot quick-hire jobs and scams

At work

Who uses this

  • Job seekers who need work soon
  • Career changers
  • People returning after caregiving or a gap

How agents work

Four parts of every agent

  1. InstructionsIts job description: goal, rules, format
  2. InputsThe files and facts you give it
  3. OutputThe table, list, or draft it gives back
  4. You checkYou are responsible for the final result

Build it

Set up your agent

  1. Open your AI assistant and turn on web search
  2. Paste the starter instructions from the Agent Labs page
  3. Fill in the [brackets] with your skills, pay, schedule, and travel
  4. Explain your must-haves in your own words

These files have mistakes in them on purpose. Look for:

  • Skills hidden in everyday life (caregiving, volunteering)
  • Must-haves like pay, schedule, and bus routes
  • Duplicate and closed postings
  • Red flags: fees, bank details, text-only interviews

Check it

Before you trust the output

  • Every match meets the must-haves
  • QUICK HIRE labels are backed by the listing
  • Scams are named and explained
  • Real openings are confirmed on the employer's site

Ethics & accountability

Use it responsibly

  • Never pay to get a job
  • Never send identification (ID), bank, or Social Security details before a real offer
  • Report scam listings to the job site
