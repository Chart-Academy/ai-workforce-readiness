Skills-to-Openings Agent

AI for Workforce Readiness · Chart Academy

The job

What you build today

You build your own Job Search Hub: a private website on your computer where your agents find real openings, rank them against your must-haves, and keep your top 10, your application tracker, your résumés, and your cover letters in one place.

How agents work

Your agent team

  1. Profile Builderturns your résumé and your own notes into a skills profile
  2. Opening Findersearches the web for real openings and adds the ones you found yourself
  3. Fit Checkerchecks every opening against your must-haves and picks your top 10
  4. Your huba back end (hub.py) and a Jobs page you design, with your tracker and documents

Build it

Paste, check, repeat

  1. Open your project in Claude Code and type git pull
  2. Open the Day 1 prompts
  3. Paste one prompt at a time, in order
  4. Change the [brackets] to your own choices
  5. Check what Claude built before you paste the next one

Check it

Before you trust the output

  • Every top job meets your must-haves, or says why not
  • Unknown is not the same as met: read the real listing
  • QUICK HIRE labels are backed by the listing
  • Scams are left out and explained
  • You open every link yourself

Ethics & accountability

Use it responsibly

  • Agents search; you apply. No agent logs in to a job site or applies for you
  • Remove your phone, email, and home address before your résumé goes to AI
  • Never pay to get a job or send ID, bank, or Social Security details before a real offer
  • Fit scores are estimates, not promises

Lab assignment · submit in Canvas

Your turn

  1. Open your job search project: follow Start of class on the Setting Up Coding for Agents page, and type git pull to get today's lab files
  2. Open the Day 1 prompts and paste them into Claude Code one at a time, in order. Start with your résumé and data/about_me.md: answer every question in your own words
  3. Build and run your Profile Builder and Opening Finder agents (Prompts 4 and 5). Check that everything in your profile is true
  4. Search Indeed and DC Government Careers yourself for two or three jobs you'd really apply to. Paste them into data/listings_pasted.txt and run the Opening Finder again
  5. Build your Fit Checker, your hub's back end, and its Jobs page (Prompts 6, 7, and 8). Fill in the [brackets] with your own choices
  6. Start your hub and check every job in your top 10: open the listing, compare it with your must-haves, save your check with a note in your own words, and add the jobs you'd apply to to your tracker
  7. Save your work to GitHub with Prompt 11
  8. Turn in: in Canvas, paste your GitHub repo link and attach a screenshot of your top 10 with your checks saved and a screenshot of your tracker
  9. Type your “In your own words” answers in Canvas. They are graded

In your own words (required, no AI for this part)

  • “The must-have that ruled out the most jobs was … because …”
  • “The agent ranked ___ too high/low. When I opened the listing, I saw …”
  • “A skill from my own past that my profile showed me I have is …”
