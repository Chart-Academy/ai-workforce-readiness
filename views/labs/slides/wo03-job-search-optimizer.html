Job Search Optimizer

AI for Workforce Readiness · Chart Academy

The job

What this agent does

  • Compare your résumé to a job posting
  • Show which skills match and which are missing
  • Keep track of every application

At work

Who uses this

  • First-time job seekers
  • People returning to work
  • Anyone applying for a promotion

How agents work

Four parts of every agent

  1. InstructionsIts job description: goal, rules, format
  2. InputsThe files and facts you give it
  3. OutputThe table, list, or draft it gives back
  4. You checkYou are responsible for the final result

Build it

Set up your agent

  1. Open your AI assistant
  2. Paste the starter instructions from the Agent Labs page
  3. Tell it in your own words what job you want and why
  4. Give it the sample résumé and job posting first, then your own

These files have mistakes in them on purpose. Look for:

  • Vague bullets and typos
  • Dates and contact info that don't look professional
  • Skills listed that aren't current
  • Duplicate or overdue applications

Check it

Before you trust the output

  • Every line describes something the person really did
  • Keywords from the posting appear where they're true
  • The tracker shows what needs action first

Ethics & accountability

Use it responsibly

  • Never let AI add jobs, titles, or skills you don't have
  • Keep your own voice in the cover letter
  • Don't share your Social Security number with AI
