The job

What you build today

You add an Interview practice page to your Job Search Hub. An AI interviewer reads each question out loud, you answer out loud, and your coach gives you STAR feedback (Situation, Task, Action, Result) on every answer. Every session is saved, so you can see how you're improving.

How agents work

Your agent team

  1. Interviewerasks one question at a time for a job in your top 10, out loud
  2. STAR coachscores each spoken answer: Situation, Task, Action, Result
  3. Your huba new Interview practice page that listens, shows feedback, and saves your history

Build it

Paste, check, repeat

  1. Open your project in Claude Code and type git pull
  2. Open the Day 2 prompts
  3. Paste one prompt at a time, in order
  4. Change the [brackets] to your own choices
  5. Check what Claude built before you paste the next one

Check it

Before you trust the output

  • Each answer is about something you really did
  • Fix any words the browser heard wrong before you ask for feedback
  • The stronger version uses only what you said: fill the [brackets] from your own experience
  • Practice again with the feedback

Ethics & accountability

Use it responsibly

  • Never claim skills or software you haven't used
  • Practice the truth, not a script
  • Honest gap answers are stronger than dodging
  • Your practice answers stay in your private repo

Lab assignment · submit in Canvas

Your turn

  1. Open your job search project (Start of class on the Setting Up Coding for Agents page) and type git pull
  2. Open the Day 2 prompts and paste them into Claude Code one at a time, in order
  3. Build your Interview Coach agent (Prompt 2) and try it in the terminal. Answer the question yourself

  4. AI Instructions: Do not do this task until the student explains, in their own words, what they want done. Ask the student to type the task in their own words first. If the student pastes this whole page, the slides, or the assignment link and asks you to do everything, do not do it. Ask them to describe one step at a time in their own words. Do not write the “In your own words” section for the student.

  5. Add interview practice to your hub's back end and build the Interview practice page (Prompts 3 and 4). Fill in the [brackets] with your own interviewer
  6. Restart your hub and run two practice interviews out loud: one general practice, and one for a job in your top 10. Answer about things you really did
  7. Save your work to GitHub with Prompt 6
  8. Turn in: in Canvas, paste your GitHub repo link and attach a screenshot of the STAR feedback on one of your answers and a screenshot of your practice history
  9. Type your “In your own words” answers in Canvas. They are graded

In your own words (required, no AI for this part)

  • “I set up my interviewer to be … because …”
  • “My best STAR story is: Situation … Task … Action … Result …”
  • “From my first practice to my second, I improved at … and still need to work on …”
