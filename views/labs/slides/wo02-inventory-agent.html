Inventory Database Agent

AI for Workforce Readiness · Chart Academy

The job

What this agent does

  • Turn a messy supply list into one clean table
  • Update counts from everyday notes
  • Flag low stock and draft a reorder

At work

Who uses this

  • Custodial and facilities teams
  • Restaurant and kitchen staff
  • Warehouses, stockrooms, and front offices

How agents work

Four parts of every agent

  1. InstructionsIts job description: goal, rules, format
  2. InputsThe files and facts you give it
  3. OutputThe table, list, or draft it gives back
  4. You checkYou are responsible for the final result

Build it

Set up your agent

  1. Open your AI assistant and a blank spreadsheet
  2. Paste the starter instructions from the Agent Labs page
  3. Change [custodial team at a school] to your workplace
  4. Tell the AI in your own words what a clean inventory should look like

Practice data · on the Agent Labs page

Your practice files

  • Download all (.zip)
  • wo02-supply-inventory-messy.csv
  • wo02-supply-usage-notes.docx

These files have mistakes in them on purpose. Look for:

  • The same item written different ways
  • Units and dates that don't match
  • Counts that aren't numbers
  • Anything unsafe

Check it

Before you trust the output

  • Every real item is still there
  • Duplicates are merged, not deleted
  • Units and dates use one format
  • Notes it couldn't record are listed, not guessed

Ethics & accountability

Use it responsibly

  • Check chemical safety on the label or Safety Data Sheet, not just AI
  • Report safety problems to a supervisor
  • Don't guess counts you can't confirm
