Lesson 6Microsoft Word+

Make documents that look professional, and use AI to draft and fix them faster.

  • Styles, headings, bullets, and tables that stay neat when you edit
  • Using AI (Copilot in Word or a chat assistant) to draft, shorten, or reword text
  • Track Changes and comments when someone reviews your work
  • Saving and sharing: clear file names and Portable Document Format (PDF) copies

Real-world problem: Your supervisor hands you a two-page safety notice. It has five different fonts, spaces instead of tabs, and three typos. It goes up in the break room tomorrow and gets emailed to 40 staff.

In-class assessment · submit in Canvas

  • lesson6-safety-notice-messy.docx
  1. Fix the layout with styles, headings, and a table instead of fixing each line by hand.
  2. Ask AI to make the wording easier to read in under a minute. Turn on Track Changes and accept or reject each change yourself.
  3. Save a final copy as a PDF for the email.

Lesson 8Résumé Writing+

Strengthen the résumé you already have, or build your first one from your work, school, volunteer, and life experience.

  • Reviewing your résumé: what works, what's missing, and what's out of date
  • Choosing a format: work history first, skills first, or a mix of both
  • Strong bullet points: action, task, and result, with numbers when you have them
  • Tailoring your résumé to a job posting without adding skills you don't have
  • Explaining gaps, and keeping the layout simple so résumé-screening software can read it

Real-world problem: your own résumé. Maybe it's out of date, lists duties instead of results, has a gap you're not sure how to explain, or doesn't exist yet. By the end of class, you'll have a stronger version and a copy tailored to a real job.

In-class assessment · submit in Canvas

  • lesson8-resume-checklist.docx
  • lesson8-resume-starter.docx
  1. Open your current résumé in Word. No résumé yet? Fill in the starter worksheet instead. Remove your address, phone number, and email before you share it with AI.
  2. Ask AI to review it using the checklist. Choose the three changes that matter most and make them yourself, including at least two stronger bullet points.
  3. Find one real job posting you want. Save a copy of your résumé and tailor it to that posting, without adding anything that isn't true.
