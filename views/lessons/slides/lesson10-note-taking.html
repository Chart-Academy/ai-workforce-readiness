Module 4 · Applied skills · Lesson 10

Note-taking and Organization

Capture what matters in meetings, trainings, and calls, and turn it into a plan you'll follow.

Learning objectives

The learner will be able to:

  1. Organize meeting notes into decisions, action items, and open questions.
  2. Use AI to summarize a meeting transcript, then correct what it got wrong.
  3. Turn action items into a to-do list with due dates and add them to a calendar.
  4. Name and file documents so they can be found later, and ask permission before recording anyone.

Real-world problem

Summary and to-do list, by end of day

What you have

training THURS 10/22 2pm swaps thru app, 24 hrs ahead me - send invites to everyone payroll ???

What you need

  • What was decided
  • Who does what, by when
  • What's still open
  • Correct dates and rules

Your notes and the transcript don't agree. One of them is wrong, and it matters for 40 coworkers.

Capture what matters

Notes in three buckets

  • Decisions

    What was agreed: “Go-live is Nov 2.”

  • Action items

    Who, what, and by when: “Kevin: poster by Fri 10/16.”

  • Open questions

    What's still unknown, and who's finding out

Write keywords, not full sentences. Mark anything you're unsure of with “?” and check it after.

Recordings and transcripts

Ask first, then let the tools listen

  • Ask before you record

    Get everyone's OK at the start. Some workplaces and states require it.

  • Built-in transcripts

    Meeting apps and phone voice memos can turn speech into text

  • Transcripts make mistakes

    Names, numbers, and dates are often heard wrong

Use AI, then check it

Sort the transcript, then compare

Ask

“From this transcript, list the decisions, the action items (who, what, by when), and the open questions. After each item, quote the line it came from.”

  • Check every name, date, and number against the transcript
  • Read the end of the meeting closely. Plans often change at the last minute (“Actually, it's 48 hours, not 24”)
  • Asking for quotes makes each item easy to check

Make it doable

A to-do list you'll actually follow

  1. CaptureEvery action item that's yours
  2. ClarifyWrite the very next step
  3. Date itAdd a real due date
  4. Schedule itPut it on your calendar

Vague

“scheduling stuff”

Clear

“Send ShiftLine login invites to all staff, by Fri 10/16, 3 pm.”

Find it later

Name and file it so you can find it

One folder, clear names

Work/ Meetings/ 2026-10-13 Staff Meeting - Summary.docx 2026-10-13 Staff Meeting - Transcript.docx Schedules/ Training/

  • Start file names with the date (year-month-day) so they sort in order
  • Say what it is in the name
  • Keep work files in one place, not scattered across email and downloads
