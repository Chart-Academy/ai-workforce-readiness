Module 4 · Applied skills · Lesson 7

Google Sheets

Keep records, do quick math, and spot problems in a spreadsheet, with AI as your helper.

Learning objectives

The learner will be able to:

  1. Sort, filter, and freeze the header row in a Google Sheet.
  2. Convert text entries to numbers and total them with SUM, AVERAGE, and IF formulas.
  3. Check a formula written by AI by hand on at least two rows.
  4. Flag problem values with a formula and build a chart a supervisor can read at a glance.

Real-world problem

Payroll is Friday. The totals are wrong.

Part of the timesheet

EmployeeMonTueWedTotal
Dana Price88840
Luis Ortega8 hrs87.5h28
Ana Silva88:30832

Google Sheets skips anything typed as text when it adds up a total, so Luis is short 15.5 hours. Small typing habits turn into real paycheck mistakes.

Get started

Open the timesheet in Google Sheets

  1. UploadGoogle Drive → New → File upload
  2. Open itDouble-click the file, then choose Open with → Google Sheets
  3. ConvertFile → Save as Google Sheets so every tool works

Google saves as you type. Use File → Version history if you need to undo a big mistake.

Get around

Spreadsheet basics

  • Cells and ranges

    B6 is one cell. B6:G6 is a row of cells from B6 to G6.

  • Sort and filter

    Data → Sort range or Data → Create a filter to find rows fast

  • Freeze the header

    View → Freeze → 1 row keeps column names in view

  • Numbers are numbers

    Numbers line up on the right. Text lines up on the left.

Clean the data

Make every number a real number

Typed as text

8 hrs · 7.5h · eight · 8:30

Left-aligned. Totals skip them.

Real numbers

8 · 7.5 · 8 · 8.5

Right-aligned. Formulas count them.

  • Fix many at once: Edit → Find and replace (Ctrl + H), replace “ hrs” with nothing
  • Make a column count as numbers: select it, then Format → Number → Number
  • Check a cell: =ISNUMBER(B6) shows TRUE for a real number

Do the math

Three formulas that cover most jobs

FormulaWhat it means
=SUM(B6:G6)Add up Monday through Saturday for row 6
=AVERAGE(H6:H14)The average weekly total
=IF(H6>40,"Overtime","")Say “Overtime” if the total is over 40, otherwise leave it blank

Type the formula once, then drag the small blue square at the corner of the cell down to copy it to every row.

Ask AI, then test

Let AI write the formula. You check it.

  1. DescribeYour columns and rows, in plain words
  2. Get itAsk for the formula and an explanation
  3. Try itPaste it into two rows
  4. CheckAdd those two rows by hand. Do they match?

Ask

“Hours for Monday through Saturday are in columns B to G, starting in row 6. Write a Google Sheets formula for the weekly total in column H, and explain each part.”

Use Gemini in Google Sheets if your account has it, or any AI assistant.

See problems fast

Flags and charts

  • Flag overtime

    In a new column: =IF(H2>40, "OVER 40", ""), then fill it down

  • Make a chart

    Insert → Chart, then choose Chart type → Bar chart for weekly totals

  • Give it a title

    Chart editor → Customize → Chart & axis titles. “Hours by employee, week of Oct 5” beats “Chart title”

Troubleshooting

Why totals come out wrong

  • Text, not numbers

    “8 hrs” is skipped

  • Range too short

    =SUM(H6:H13) misses row 14

  • Typed over a formula

    A plain 32 where a formula should be

  • Same person twice

    “Dana Price” and “Dana Price ” with a space
