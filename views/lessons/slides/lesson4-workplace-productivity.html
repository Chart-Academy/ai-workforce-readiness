Workplace Productivity

Everyday tasks where AI saves real time.

Email

Drafting and replying in a professional tone

  1. PasteThe email you got, with private details removed
  2. SayThe main point of your reply, in your words
  3. Pick a toneFriendly, direct, or apologetic
  4. EditFix facts and make it sound like you

Try it

“Reply to this email from my supervisor. Tell her I can cover Saturday but not Sunday because of a family event. Keep it friendly and under 4 sentences.”

Summaries

Long documents and meeting notes, made short

Ask

“Summarize this email thread in 5 bullet points. End with a list of who needs to do what, and by when.”

Then check it against the original:

  • Names and dates
  • Numbers and amounts
  • Who agreed to do what
  • Anything important it left out

Summaries drop details. The missing one is often the one that matters.

Work once, reuse often

Templates you can reuse every week

Save this in a document

You are my assistant at [workplace]. Summarize these [shift notes] in [5] bullet points for [my supervisor]. Then list any problems that need action, with who and when.

  • Shift handoff note

    What happened and what's next

  • Weekly schedule

    Shifts, appointments, deadlines

  • Status update

    A short email to your supervisor

Each week, swap the [brackets] and paste in the new notes.
